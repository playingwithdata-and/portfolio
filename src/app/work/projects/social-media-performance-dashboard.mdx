## Overview

Not all metrics matter. This dashboard was built to cut through the noise — tracking only the KPIs that actually correlate with business outcomes: engagement rate by platform, audience demographic shifts, content type performance, and posting time optimization.

## What it does

- **Engagement tracking** — cross-platform comparison of likes, shares, comments, and saves, normalized per 1000 impressions
- **Audience analysis** — demographic breakdowns that update as follower bases shift; catches micro-trends before they become macro problems
- **Content performance** — categorizes posts by format (image, video, carousel, text) and measures which formats drive the most meaningful engagement
- **Time-of-day optimization** — heatmaps showing when each audience segment is most active

## Tech

- **Tableau** — interactive dashboards with parameter-driven filtering
- **Excel** — data cleaning and preprocessing before ingestion
- **SQL** — querying platform APIs and internal databases for raw data

## What I learned

The insight that surprised me most: our highest-engagement content type (short-form video) was not our highest-converting content type (long-form educational carousels). The dashboard made this visible, and it changed our content calendar.
